# Diez42 v315.0

Spanish-first ES/EN static expansion based on the organization brief supplied on 11 November 2026. Includes the 14 program catalog and detail pages, exact schedules, join buttons, eight QR images generated from the supplied WhatsApp URLs, weekly printable schedule, multilingual welcome/help summaries, mission/vision/inclusion statement, projects, giving, volunteer and team enquiry forms, partners and legal/privacy/cookie pages.

The supplied football MOV was matched to the pCloud share, converted with AVFoundation to an approximately 44 MB MP4, and given a poster frame extracted at two seconds. Football players are shown only in the user-supplied video. Existing program and hero photography is AI-generated. Original source media remains unchanged.

Forms prepare a WhatsApp message locally. The visitor reviews and sends it in WhatsApp; there is no form backend. The public site no longer loads the aggregate analytics beacon. The cookie notice explains local preference storage; optional third-party embeds are not loaded.

Pending information is explicitly marked: Mariposa description, food eligibility, unspecified participant requirements, contact email and social accounts, fundraising progress and confirmed expansion amounts, consented stories, full registration details and final privacy/legal review. No donation tax claims are made.

Source content: `data/programs.json`; page builder: `scripts/build_content.py`; homepage template: `templates/home.html`. Run `python3 scripts/build_content.py`, then `python3 scripts/check_release.py` after editing content. QR images are static PNGs; when a join link changes, regenerate its QR and scan/decode it against the new URL.

Validation: 31 public pages and local destinations checked; 14 program details; QR URLs independently decoded using Apple Vision; ES/EN persistence, mobile program grid and schedule inspected; volunteer form validation and WhatsApp message preparation verified without sending a message. Football source and poster are local and playable with native controls, muted by default, no autoplay. Public release remains subject to pending information review.
