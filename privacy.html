Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains how the Diez42 website handles visitor information.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

This site does not have accounts, contact forms, payments, donations, or its own backend. It counts aggregate page views and button presses to improve and optimize the site, and directs visitors to confirmed contact channels.

Site measurement

Web Signals Tracker receives aggregate page-view and call-to-action press events. Measurement creates no visitor or session identifier, collects no form contents, and is suppressed when the browser signals Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Information we store

The site stores your language and theme preferences in your browser's local storage. That information stays on your device and is used only to remember your selection on future visits.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is published through GitHub Pages. Like any web hosting service, GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and access time to deliver the site and protect the service.

External links

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook open external services. If you choose to use them, those services may process your data under their own privacy policies.

Website imagery

Hero scenes are AI-generated illustrations inspired by Diez42 activities. They illustrate the work and are not records of specific people or events. For questions about imagery, contact Diez42.

Contact

For privacy questions or to contact Diez42, use the published channels on the homepage: WhatsApp, phone, or the listed mailing address.
